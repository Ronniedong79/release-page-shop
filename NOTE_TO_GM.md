# A note for the General Manager

I've built the online shop for your limited releases. Customers log in, see what's in stock and what it costs right now, and buy it, either straight away or through a cart. Your team has its own login to add new items and change stock and prices. Both problems from your message are now blocked by the shop itself, not by the web page, so tricks in the browser don't work. The price is always taken from our own records at the moment of purchase, and anything the browser says about the price is ignored. Every customer is limited to two of each item across all their orders. The shop checks this even if many orders arrive at the same instant, and it records exactly what each customer paid. One thing it doesn't do yet is take payment: orders are recorded, but there is no card payment step or order cancellation.
